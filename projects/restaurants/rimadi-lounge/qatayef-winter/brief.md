# Rimadi Lounge (رمادي لاونج) – Winter Qatayef Box ad

- **Goal:** winter ad for the qatayef box; one warm gathering around the box while the world changes around it.
- **Approach:** image-to-video from the real box + logo photos (no shoot). People and environments are AI-generated;
  the box and logo are the real product pixels (placed + locked).
- **Looks:** 1) winter desert camp (tent + fire) 2) warm home majlis 3) elegant outdoor istiraha.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, social (TikTok / Reels / Snapchat). Arabic RTL titles.
- **Stage now:** 5-second test only (≈1.7 s per look, light-wipe between them). Full ad only after review.
- **Must keep:** box shape, colours, logo, any text on the box. No real people's likeness.
- **Budget cap for the test:** to be approved by the client (estimate in budget.md).
