# What to provide (put files in projects/restaurants/rimadi-lounge/qatayef-winter/assets/ – git-ignored, never uploaded without approval)

1. **box_front.png** – the box straight-on, **cut out with a transparent background** (PNG with alpha). This is the
   exact pixels that will appear in the ad. 2000 px wide or more.
2. **box_34.jpg** – three-quarter view (front + side visible), on a plain background.
3. **box_top.jpg** – top view (lid / logo), plain background.
4. **box_open.jpg** (optional) – box open with qatayef inside, if the ad should show them.
5. **logo.png / logo.svg** – the Rimadi Lounge logo, transparent background.
6. **brand.md** (optional) – brand colours (hex), fonts, slogan, Arabic copy you want, things to avoid.

Photo tips: soft even daylight or a light tent, no coloured reflections, logo sharp and readable, camera at table
height for the 3/4 view (matches a seated eye-line in the scenes). If you only have phone photos, that is fine;
the cut-out can be made locally.
